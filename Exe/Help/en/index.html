Contents

Coins Collection keeps your whole coin collection on your computer — in one place, from two sources: modern coins from uCoin (import of the uCoin export file) and coins that uCoin does not have, such as ancient, Roman or Celtic coins, from Numista. The program finds every coin in the Numista catalogue, loads mintage, details and pictures, and can add your coins to your Numista collection.

Coin and My item

  • Coin — data of the coin type: country, period, currency, denomination, year (a range — the second field; before Christ — a negative year, -44), variety, diameter, catalogue number, subject. They are shared by all items of this coin; if there are several items, the card warns you.
  • Numista search — your own search phrase for Numista. Empty — an automatic phrase is used (shown in grey): denomination, period, year. See Numista: linking coins.
  • My item — your copy: condition, quantity, prices, dates, swap, comment, grading.

Photo

Obverse and reverse: File..., Paste (from the clipboard), Remove, or drag a file from Explorer onto the frame. See Photos.

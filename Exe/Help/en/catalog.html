Numista catalogue

The node Numista catalogue at the top of the tree shows all coins of the Numista catalogue for a country, an issuer or a Roman emperor — not only yours. You see at once what you have and what you are missing.

Where the countries come from

The tree is built from the list of Numista issuers (it is loaded once with the Numista lists: Numista → Connect / Settings): continents, Extinct states, then the Numista countries and their issuers — for example Switzerland → Swiss cantons → Basel or Central and Eastern Europe (ancient) → Celts. Opening the tree costs no requests. Fantasy states and micronations are not shown.

Roman Empire is one issuer on Numista; in the program it is divided into dynasties and emperors (from Augustus to Theodosius I).

Loading the coins

Choose an issuer or an emperor and press Load from Numista. One request loads 100 types: a small issuer — 1–2 requests, a big emperor — 10–30. If more than 10 requests are needed, the program asks first. Loaded types stay on your computer: choosing the node again costs nothing, also without internet. Refresh from Numista loads the list again (the program suggests it after a week: new types appear on Numista).

The list

  • Column Mine: ✓ — in your collection, ⇄ — in the swap list, ★ — in the wish list, empty — you do not have it. Only coins linked with Numista are recognized — link your coins first (Numista: linking coins).
  • Only missing — only the types you do not have; By century — grouped by century.
  • Click a column header to sort; the search field above the list searches the titles.
  • Right-click a type: Load type details from Numista (1 request by the N#, no search) — composition, weight, size, ruler and other details appear below; Open on Numista.
  • Double-click — the type page on Numista. The details below show the type; if the type is linked with one of your coins — its characteristics and catalogue photos.
The catalogue is read-only: countries cannot be dragged into it or out of it.

How to link a coin — typical cases

Find the case that looks like yours: what you see — what to do — where to read more.

1. The coin has a KM#, Y# or C# number

You see: the number in the uCoin number row of the details.
Do: check the coins and press Auto-link. Most such coins are linked at once.
More: Numista: linking coins.

2. uCoin number (UC#), commemorative coin

You see: UC# 428 instead of KM#. Numista does not know uCoin numbers.
Do: auto-link searches by the phrase country + year + subject. Commemorative coins of one year differ only by the subject — make sure the Subject field is filled in the coin card.
More: Numista: linking coins, section Coins with a uCoin number.

3. Nothing is found

You see: auto-link counts the coin as not found; the type list is empty.
Why: sometimes Numista keeps the catalogue number not at the type but at its issues (example: Russian Empire 2 kopecks Y# 10 — on Numista it is one type 2 Kopecks — Alexander II / III / Nicholas II), or names differ (uCoin kopeks, Numista Kopecks).
Do:

  1. Find the coin on the Numista website: search by country and denomination, compare the pictures.
  2. Copy the type title from its page (for example 2 Kopecks - Alexander II / III / Nicholas II).
  3. In the program: Link manually, paste the title into Search phrase, press Search and choose the type. The phrase is saved in the coin card.

4. Many years of one coin

You see: the same coin of different years (1 kopiyka 2005, 2006…), all with the same number.
Do: link one coin. The program offers to link the other years at once (if they fit the years of the type); auto-link also takes the type from the linked coin — without new requests. Each coin gets the issue of its own year.
More: Numista: linking coins, section Coins of one number from different years.

5. Historical state

You see: the coin is found only under another issuer (Poland 1766 → Polish–Lithuanian Commonwealth).
Do: confirm the type in the list. The issuer is remembered for the period, the next coins of the period are found at once.
More: Numista: linking coins, section Historical states.

6. The issue is not identified

You see: #Numista is grey, no mintage. Numista has several issues for the year (different mints, Proof).
Do: fill in the mint in Variety (for example SPB, EM) and link again, or choose it yourself: Choose issue.

7. Linked with the wrong type

Do: Unlink from Numista or simply Link manually again. If the coin is already uploaded to your Numista collection, the program asks whether to change the item there too.
More: Numista: linking coins, section Wrong link.

8. Ancient coins and coins that uCoin does not have

Do: add them on the Numista website to your collection, then Numista → Import from my Numista collection. See Where to start, way 2.

Numista: mintage and type details

For linked coins the program keeps data from the Numista catalogue:

  • Mintage of the issue — the Mintage column (n/a — Numista has no mintage);
  • Type details — composition (the Composition column), weight, size, shape, technique, ruler, series, commemorated topic, years, mints, demonetization (coin details, right column);
  • Photos of the type (Photos).

Update catalogue data

Check coins → Update catalogue data: loads what is missing. Cached answers are free, so a repeated run usually needs 0 requests.

One coin: right-click → Update catalogue data (no cache) — requests the data again (2 requests), for example when a mintage was added on Numista.

Numista prices

Numista estimates the price of an issue for each condition (G, VG, F, VF, XF, AU, UNC). The program can load these estimates:

  1. Turn them on: Numista settings, tab What to load from Numista, Numista prices (estimate by condition); choose the currency (UAH, USD or EUR).
  2. One coin: right-click → Load Numista prices. Many: check coins → Numista prices... — the confirmation shows how many requests are needed; afterwards the check marks are removed from the coins that got prices (coins without an issue stay checked).
  3. Or without a separate command: Load together with type details (or the third choice in the auto-link confirmation). Then auto-link (with details), Link manually, Choose issue, Update catalogue data and the import from your Numista collection load the price of the issue too (+1 request per issue without a fresh price); the result window shows how many prices were loaded.
  • The column Numista, UAH shows the estimate for the condition of your coin; empty — no estimate for this condition (or the issue is not chosen). The coin details show the estimates of all conditions, your condition in [brackets], and the date.
  • 1 request per issue; coins of one issue cost one request. Prices younger than the number of days in the settings (90 by default) are not requested again; for one coin — only prices loaded today are skipped.
  • Only coins with a chosen issue get prices — Choose issue.
  • Prices in another currency stay in the database: switching the currency back shows them again.

Auction sales

Numista collects past sales of coins at auctions (the Past sales block on the type page). For a linked coin: right-click → Auction sales... (or click the Numista prices row in the details).

  • The list: date, auction house, auction and lot, condition, price in the sale currency and in the currency of the price settings (converted by Numista at the historical rate; “—” — no rate), whether the buyer's premium is included. Double-click opens the lot.
  • My issue — sales of the coin's issue (year, mint); Whole type — of all years. Only my condition — sales in the condition of your coin.
  • Above the list: how many sales (and in the last 3 years) and the prices — from, to, median. Real sales are a good addition to the Numista estimate; the price may or may not include the buyer's premium.
  • 1 request (up to 500 sales; more — up to 3 requests, the newest 500 are shown). The answer is kept for 7 days; Refresh asks Numista again. Sales are not saved in the collection; pictures of lots are not shown — open the lot.

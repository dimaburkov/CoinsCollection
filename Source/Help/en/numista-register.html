Numista: registration and API key

Numista is free. The program works with Numista through the official Numista API: each user has their own free key (2000 requests per month — enough for a collection of several hundred coins). Registration takes about 5 minutes.

1. Register on Numista

  1. Open en.numista.com and press Sign up.
  2. Enter a user name, your e-mail and a password.
  3. Confirm your e-mail: open the letter from Numista and follow the link.

2. Get your API key

  1. Signed in to Numista, open en.numista.com/api/api_key.php.
  2. Fill in the short form. In the description of use you can write: personal management of my coin collection with the Coins Collection program.
  3. Copy the key that Numista gives you. Keep it private, like a password.

3. Enter the key in the program

  1. Numista → Connect / Settings, paste the key.
  2. Press Check connection: the program checks the key and loads the lists of Numista issuers and catalogues (once).

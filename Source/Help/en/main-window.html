Main window

Navigation tree

On the left: All, continents with their countries, No continent, Extinct states and Checked (all checked coins from any country, see Checking coins). The number in brackets is the number of items.

Search

The search field on the toolbar filters the list by country, period, currency, denomination, year, subject, variety, catalogue number and comment.

Coin list

ColumnMeaning
☐Check box for mass operations (Checking coins).
Country, Period, Currency, DenominationFrom uCoin. Country is hidden when one country is selected in the tree.
SubjectSubject of a commemorative coin (from uCoin or the coin card); shown instead of Country when one country is selected. The column takes the free width of the list; after you change its width with the mouse, it keeps your width.

✓The item is uploaded to your Numista collection.
RarityRarity index from your Numista collection comment (Numista: upload).
MintageMintage of the issue. n/a — Numista has no mintage for it; empty — not loaded yet.
CompositionMetal of the type from Numista.
Year, Condition, ValueFrom uCoin.

Click a column header to sort, click it again to reverse. By default the list is sorted by year, then by denomination.

Coin details

Under the list: obverse and reverse photos (double-click — photo viewer) and the coin data in two columns. The #Numista value is a link to the type page on Numista. The right column shows the type details loaded from Numista (weight, size, shape, ruler, series, years, mints, demonetization).

Status bar

Number of shown items, their quantity and value, and the number of checked items.

Photos

Item photo and coin type photo

  • An item photo is your own shot of your copy.
  • A coin type photo is shared by all items of the coin (usually the picture from the Numista catalogue).
  • The coin card shows both side by side: coin type on the left, my photo on the right — a poor photo of a worn coin can be compared with the catalogue at once.
  • The coin details show the item photo; without it — the coin type photo, marked coin type. If the coin has both, the button ⇄ in the corner of the obverse switches between your photo and the catalogue; the choice stays while you move through the list.

Adding photos

In the coin card: File... (JPEG, PNG, BMP, GIF, TIFF), Paste, or drag a file onto the frame. Photos are reduced to 1200 px and saved as JPEG in Photos\.

Photos from Numista

When a coin is linked with Numista, the obverse and reverse pictures of the type are downloaded automatically as coin type photos (linking, update catalogue data). Your own coin type photo is never replaced. The picture author is shown in the viewer: Photo: <author> / Numista.

Viewer

Double-click a photo in the coin details. If the coin has both your photos and catalogue photos, the buttons My photo / Catalogue (or ↑/↓) switch between them. Mouse wheel — zoom, drag — move, ←/→ or Space — other side, 0 or Home — fit to window, Esc — close.

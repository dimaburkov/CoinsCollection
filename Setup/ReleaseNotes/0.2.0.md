## Coins Collection 0.2.0 — first public version

Your coin collection on your computer — and an easy way to move it from **uCoin** to **Numista**.

### Download

**`CoinsCollection-Setup-0.2.0.exe`** (below, in *Assets*) — Windows 10 / 11, no administrator rights needed.

The installer is not digitally signed: if the browser says *“not commonly downloaded”*, choose **Keep**; if Windows SmartScreen appears, choose **More info → Run anyway**.

SHA-256: `FFCBE65E175611FBBB8CB7D2211C0F084FAD650E6C52A4D7C05E806D4EEA61B1`

### What it can do

- Import your collection from the **uCoin** export file (`.xlsx`); a repeated import keeps your Numista links and photos.
- Find coins in the **Numista** catalogue — by KM / Y / C number, year or your own search phrase; historical states (e.g. Polish–Lithuanian Commonwealth) are remembered for the period.
- **Upload** coins to your Numista collection (condition, price, date, comment), one by one or all checked at once.
- Load **mintage, type details** (composition, weight, size, ruler, demonetization) and **obverse / reverse pictures** from Numista.
- **Import coins you added on Numista yourself** (e.g. ancient coins), with year ranges (`174–179`) and BC years.
- **Rarity index** from the private comment of your Numista items (`R:97` or simply `97`).
- Your own **photos**, photo viewer, search, tree of continents and countries, check boxes for mass operations with a progress window.
- **Backup / restore** of the whole collection (database + photos) in one `.zip` file, with a reminder.
- English and Ukrainian interface, built-in help (F1).

### First steps

1. uCoin: export your collection to `.xlsx` → **File → Import from uCoin (.xlsx)**.
2. Numista: get your free API key at https://en.numista.com/api/api_key.php → **Numista → Connect / Settings**.
3. **Auto-link** checked coins (or **Link manually**) → **Upload**.

Each user uses their own free Numista key (2000 requests per month).
